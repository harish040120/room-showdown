      Works offline. Same rules as multiplayer: 5 lives each, last one standing wins.

      How to Play

      Everyone is locked in one room with a pistol. Each player has 5 lives. Every bullet that hits you costs 1 life, then you blink for 1.5 seconds and can't be hit. Lose all 5 and you're out (you can keep watching). Last player standing wins!

      The match lasts 3 minutes. If time runs out, the player with the most lives wins (ties: most hits landed).

      Keyboard + mouse

      • WASD or arrow keys: move
      • Mouse: aim
      • Left click: shoot (hold to keep firing)
      • R: reload (6 bullets; reloads by itself when empty)
      • Esc: menu (the match keeps going!)

      Phone (landscape)

      • Left thumb: move stick
      • Right thumb: aim stick, push it past the dashed ring to fire
      • Round button: reload

      Gamepad

      • Sticks move and aim, RT/RB/A shoots, X reloads

      Tip: bullets bounce off nothing. Use the blocks for cover!
